Book Marketing & Author Services

  1. 01

    Author & Book Positioning

    Develop a clear and compelling identity for your book and author brand. Define what makes your book distinctive, identify its ideal readership, and communicate its value in a way that helps potential readers understand why it deserves their attention.

  2. 02

    Book Marketing & Promotion

    Develop practical, audience-focused marketing strategies that increase your book’s visibility and connect it with potential readers. From identifying promotional opportunities to creating engaging campaigns, build a stronger path for introducing your book, generating interest, and attracting the right audience.

  3. 03

    Book Launch Strategy

    Create a structured launch plan that gives your book a stronger start in the marketplace. Coordinate pre-launch preparation, audience awareness, promotional activities, launch-day engagement, and post-launch opportunities to sustain interest and encourage continued readership.

  4. 04

    Digital Strategy

    Build a connected digital presence that supports your overall book marketing objectives. Align your website, landing pages, content, social platforms, and promotional channels to create a consistent experience that guides potential readers from discovering your work to exploring and purchasing your books.

  5. 05

    Author Websites

    Establish a professional online home that represents your identity, showcases your publications, and makes it easier for readers to connect with your work. Bring your biography, books, media features, upcoming projects, and relevant links together in one accessible platform that strengthens your credibility and supports your long-term author brand.

  6. 06

    Book Landing Pages

    Create dedicated, conversion-focused pages designed around individual books, launches, or promotional campaigns. Present your book description, cover, author information, reader benefits, reviews, and purchase links in a clear and engaging format that helps visitors understand your book and take the next step.

Questions authors ask

What does a book marketing and launch consultant do?

A book marketing and launch consultant plans how a book reaches its readers. That covers positioning, launch planning, promotion, and the website or landing page where interested readers take their next step.

When should I start planning my book launch?

Ideally well before publication, so there is time to clarify positioning and build anticipation. If your book is already out, a review of your positioning and book page can still improve how readers find and respond to it.

Do you build author websites and book landing pages?

Yes. Author websites bring your story, books, links and email sign-up into one place. Book landing pages focus on one book, launch or campaign with a single clear action for the reader.
